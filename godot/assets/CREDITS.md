## Map tile atlas (`data/map/tile_atlas.png`)

Built by `tools/build_tile_atlas.gd`; every pixel is recoloured to `MapPalette.DAY`.

- From Micro Roguelike (sheet column, row): `house` and `house_lit` (5, 7), `tower` (6, 7), `barricade` (12, 2).
- Generated in the project's own style: every ground tile (all kinds and variants), `capital`, `road`, `paved`, `rail`, `smoke_0`, `smoke_1`, `smoke_2`.
