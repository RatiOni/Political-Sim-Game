# Unassisted baseline playtest packet

This packet produces the human baseline needed before Ardenia's next product decision. Its purpose is to observe how people encounter the current campaign without prior explanation: what they think their role is, what they try to do, where they stop, and what makes the experience matter or fail to matter.

It is a research packet, not a demonstration script. Do not use it to prove that a planned reform, pacing model, or interface change is correct.

## The three-stage flow

1. **Prepare.** Read [the baseline protocol](BASELINE_PROTOCOL.md), recruit eligible people, and make a session copy of [the session template](SESSION_TEMPLATE.md) for each person.
2. **Observe.** Run and record 5–8 separate, uncoached external human sessions. Cover both government and opposition openings. The observer records behavior and quotations but does not teach, direct, or rescue play.
3. **Decide.** After all eligible sessions have notes, make one copy of the protocol's post-sample synthesis section. It records the pacing, first-prototype, political-counterparty, and diagnosis decisions that follow from the evidence.

## Participant standard and evidence gate

The baseline requires **5–8 external human players**: people outside the Ardenia project and its internal design/review process who have not read the Early Access mission and have not received coaching on how to play Ardenia. Each counted session also requires consent to retain anonymized notes. A developer, facilitator, agent, automated run, expert review, or developer self-test is not a participant session.

Automated checks, agent cold reads, and expert reviews may supplement the record, for example by identifying a reproducible defect. They cannot count toward, replace, or be combined with the required human-session total.

Keep raw session notes anonymous. Store them outside this public repository unless the participant has explicitly consented to their inclusion. Use a participant code such as `P01`; do not collect names, email addresses, or other unnecessary personal information.

## Hard stop

No thin reform prototype, time-model implementation, or generic decision-stop framework begins until the required human sessions **and** the post-sample synthesis exist. The baseline is a decision checkpoint, not a parallel paperwork task.

The packet records no participant results in advance. A blank template, an incomplete session, or a non-human review does not satisfy this checkpoint.
